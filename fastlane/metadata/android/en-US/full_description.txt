RAmpMusic is a multilingual Android application for streaming music from YouTube Music, available in several languages.
<br>Based on RiMusic and extending the functionality of ViMusic, enhanced with continuous playback engine improvements and modern optimizations.

## ✨ Features
- **Multilingual support:** English, Italian, German, Russian, French, Spanish, Czech, Turkish, Romanian and more. Contributions for other languages are welcome
- UI Mode, change style between RAmpMusic, RiMusic and classic ViMusic style
- Play songs from YouTube Music with uninterrupted streaming
- Change your favorite language from settings
- Auto cache songs for offline playback with custom cache size
- Download songs or entire playlist for offline playback
- Background playback with task manager swipe options
- Minimized player optimizations
- Listening statistics
- Audio visualizer on player with multiple effect types
- News, mood and musical genres
- New albums from favorite artists
- Search for songs, albums, artists and playlists
- Bookmark artists and albums
- Import online playlists
- Local playlist management
- Reorder songs in playlist or queue
- Persistent queue & quick clear
- Share menu support for YouTube and YouTube Music links
- Fetch, display, edit and translate synchronized, or not, song lyrics
- Light, Dark, Dynamic, PureBlack and ModernBack themes
- Sleep timer with custom duration
- Audio normalization & skip silence
- Android Auto support
- Android TV support
- Check for Updates, enable/disable it in settings
